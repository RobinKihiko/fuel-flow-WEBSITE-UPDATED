Questions

How do customers pay with M-Pesa?

They pay at the pump and the payment is matched to the pump, shift and branch automatically.

What is eTIMS and do I need it?

eTIMS is KRA's electronic tax invoicing system. If your station needs KRA-compliant e-invoicing, choose the Premium plan.

Can I manage more than one station?

Yes. Manage unlimited stations and branches from a single dashboard.

Can I see what each attendant sold?

Yes. You can view each attendant's daily sales and shift attendance, and spot discrepancies quickly.
